boundary
  Between the no-follow `os.stat(component, dir_fd=..., follow_symlinks=False)`
  of an INTERMEDIATE path component and the descriptor-relative
  `os.open(component, directory_flags, dir_fd=...)` of that same component, the
  component is replaced with a symlink pointing outside the artifact root.
  This is the intermediate component, not the leaf and not the root.
